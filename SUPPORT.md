## Frequently Misunderstood Issues

### "CompliPay says my invoice is valid, so I'm compliant, right?"

**No.** CompliPay validates technical compliance (XML structure, signatures, QR codes). It does NOT validate:
- Whether the tax rate is correct for your goods/services
- Whether you're eligible for exemptions
- Whether the transaction is legitimate
- Whether your business is properly registered

### "Can you tell me if this transaction is taxable?"

**No.** Tax determination requires:
- Understanding of your specific business
- Knowledge of the goods/services involved
- Awareness of applicable exemptions
- Professional tax expertise

**Consult a licensed tax advisor.**

### "ZATCA rejected my invoice. Can you help?"

**Partially.** We can help with:
- Technical rejection reasons (malformed XML, invalid signature)
- Configuration issues
- Software bugs

We CANNOT help with:
- Policy-based rejections
- Registration issues
- Compliance status disputes
- Penalty negotiations
