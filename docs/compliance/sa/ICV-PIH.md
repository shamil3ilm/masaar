# Saudi Arabia — the invoice counter (ICV) and the hash chain (PIH)

Audience: engineers working on issuing, the chain or tills, and reviewers
checking how the counter and chain meet ZATCA's rules.

In the XML every invoice has an Invoice Counter Value, KSA-16 (BR-KSA-33), of
digits only (BR-KSA-34), and a Previous Invoice Hash, KSA-13 (BR-KSA-61), that
is a base64 SHA-256 hash (BR-KSA-26) (XML Implementation Standard v1.2). How
each hash is computed is in [HASHING-AND-SIGNING.md](HASHING-AND-SIGNING.md).

## ZATCA's rules

From the E-Invoicing Detailed Guidelines:

- "An E-Invoice Solution may contain one or more Units" (p9, 2.7); a CSID
  identifies a unit (p10, 2.8 and 2.10), and "every device issuing invoices
  with the same VAT number should be registered" (FAQ, p59).
- "The E-Invoice Solution unit must not generate more than one sequence so
  that all invoices generated by an E-Invoice Solution Unit are linked using
  'Previous Invoice Hash' value into a single chain" (p28).
- The counter cannot be reset and is protected from users (p25, 5.4).
- The invoice reference number may differ, "for example, a different
  sequential reference number for each branch" (p11, 2.14).
- "Every document shall have its own hash and counter value. Rejected
  document's hash and counter value should not be changed or updated" (FAQ,
  p61).

So:

- **One counter and one chain per device**, shared by every document type —
  standard and simplified invoices, credit and debit notes alike.
- **A certificate belongs to a device**, never to a user.
- **A rejected document keeps its counter and stays in the chain**; the next
  document's PIH is its hash.

```
ICV 1  standard invoice     PIH = genesis
ICV 2  simplified invoice   PIH = hash(1)
ICV 3  credit note          PIH = hash(2)   (2 rejected: still the predecessor)
```

## Units (tills)

Every device is a row of `egs_units` (`App\Domains\Organization\Models\EgsUnit`):
head office's under the organization's own id with no branch, each branch's
first till under the branch's id, later tills under ids of their own.
`invoices.device_id` refers to the unit; its `host` is `cloud` for now.

An invoice is issued on the till its request named (`till_id`, or
`till_number` within `branch_id`), else its branch's default till, else head
office's. A branch till without a certificate of its own issues on head
office's device and in head office's sequence. Each till is onboarded with its
own OTP (`Onboarding/UnitOnboarding`); its CSR serial is the unit's
`egs_serial`. A till is retired (`Onboarding/UnitRetirement`) only once nothing
it signed is left to file; until then it is `retiring` and issues nothing new,
and the issuing step asks again under the till's lock.
`TillChainTest` and `TillConformanceTest` (through ZATCA's SDK) hold two tills
of one branch interleaving with a chain each.

## One lock for counter, number and chain

`App\Domains\Invoice\Services\IssuingLifecycle` holds the device's issuing
lock (a row of `issuing_locks`, scope from the Saudi step's `lockScope()`) for
the whole issue. Inside it, in order:

1. **ICV** — `Issuing/IssuingStep::beforeNumbering()` takes
   `Invoice::generateNextIcv()` for the device.
2. **Number and moment of issue** — set by the lifecycle.
3. **PIH and signed document** — the step reads the predecessor's hash,
   builds and signs; the lifecycle saves hash, QR, document and status in one
   update.
4. **Chain entry** — `Issuing/ChainRecorder` records the hash, its
   predecessor and the certificate that signed it; a document queued across a
   renewal is filed with that certificate version.

Two issuances on one device cannot claim the same predecessor; two devices
issue at once. There is no separate counter store: a failure rolls the counter
back with everything else, so an abandoned draft takes no number. Tills number
from their own series by default (`Invoice/Services/InvoiceNumbering`, e.g.
`INV-T01-{YYYY}-{SEQ}`); an organization choosing one shared series has its
tills wait for each other for the number. `fatoora:verify-hash-chain` checks a
chain end to end.

## Branches with their own commercial registration

ZATCA's schematron says a seller with several commercial registrations
"should fill the commercial registration of the branch in respect of which the
Tax Invoice is being issued" (BR-KSA-08). **The platform does not yet:** the
seller ID and address on every document are the organization's
(`organizations.cr_number`, `Organization::getAddressData()`), whichever branch
issues it.

## Checklist

- [x] ICV increments by one per document of any type, never reused
- [x] PIH is the immediately previous document's hash, rejected ones included
- [x] One CSID, ICV sequence and PIH chain per till; several tills per branch
- [x] A retired till never issues again
- [ ] A branch's own commercial registration and address on its invoices
