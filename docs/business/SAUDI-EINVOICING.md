# E-invoicing in Saudi Arabia, for business owners

Audience: owners and finance managers of businesses registered for VAT in Saudi Arabia, and the sales staff and partners who advise them.

This page is a plain-language summary, not tax advice. The official sources are named in brackets so you can check them:

- **[DG]** ZATCA, *Detailed Guidelines for E-Invoicing* (Fatoora), version 2
- **[TIG]** ZATCA, *Guideline for Tax Invoicing and Records under VAT Provisions*, version 3

## Who must comply

Every taxable person resident in the Kingdom, and anyone who issues tax invoices on its behalf [DG §3.1]. ZATCA, the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, runs the programme and can impose penalties for non-compliance [DG §1.4].

## Phase 1 and Phase 2

| | Phase 1: Generation | Phase 2: Integration |
|---|---|---|
| What it means | Invoices must be generated by a compliant electronic solution. Handwritten invoices, or paper invoices scanned afterwards, do not count [DG §4.2.1] | Your solution is also connected to ZATCA's Fatoora platform, and every invoice goes to ZATCA [DG §1.3] |
| Who | Everyone in scope | Taxpayers in groups ("waves") that ZATCA chooses by its own criteria. ZATCA notifies each group at least six months in advance [DG §1.3] |
| What changes on the invoice | Required fields, and a QR code on simplified invoices | XML format, a cryptographic stamp, a QR code with more data, an invoice counter and a link to the previous invoice's hash |

**What to do when you receive your wave notification:** plan to be connected before your date. On the platform, connecting takes one step: an administrator enters the one-time code (OTP) from the Fatoora portal. The platform then obtains your certificate and passes ZATCA's compliance checks.

## Two kinds of invoice

| | Standard tax invoice (business to business) | Simplified tax invoice (mostly business to consumer) |
|---|---|---|
| Used for | Sales to businesses | Sales to consumers. Also allowed for a B2B sale under SAR 1,000 [DG §4.2.1] |
| ZATCA's role | **Clearance.** ZATCA validates the invoice and stamps it *before* you give it to the buyer [DG §4.1.2] | **Reporting.** You give the buyer the invoice straight away, then report it to ZATCA **within 24 hours** [DG §4.2.2] |
| What the buyer receives | The cleared XML, or a PDF/A-3 with the XML inside [DG §4.1.2] | Usually a printed receipt with the QR code. An electronic copy if both sides agree [DG §4.2.2] |

A credit or debit note refers to the invoice it corrects, and it is the same type as that invoice [DG §4.3].

## What changes day to day

- **Invoices cannot be deleted or edited once issued.** A correction is a credit or debit note [DG §6.5].
- **Arabic is required.** Invoices must be issued in Arabic, and may also be in another language. Proper names may stay in Latin letters [TIG §4.2.1].
- **Keep records in the Kingdom for at least six years.** Records must be kept electronically, inside the Kingdom, for at least six years from the end of the tax period [TIG §8.3.1, §8.3.3]. Real-estate records must be kept for at least 15 years [TIG §8.3.3].
- **If ZATCA is unreachable,** keep trying, keep evidence of each attempt, and use ZATCA's form if a simplified invoice cannot be reported within 24 hours [DG §10].

## What the platform does for you

| Requirement | What the platform does |
|---|---|
| Compliant solution (Phase 1) | Issues correctly numbered invoices with the Phase 1 QR code, even before you connect to ZATCA |
| Connection (Phase 2) | Runs onboarding from the portal. Each till or device can have its own certificate, and you are warned before a certificate expires |
| Standard invoices | Sends each one for clearance and keeps ZATCA's stamped copy |
| Simplified invoices | Signs and prints at once, then reports in the background. The 24-hour deadline is tracked for every invoice |
| ZATCA's rules | Checks them before the invoice takes its number, so a mistake never leaves a gap in your numbering |
| No deletion | Issued invoices are locked, and corrections are made with credit and debit notes. A note is issued only once ZATCA has accepted the original, and credit notes never come to more than the invoice and its debit notes |
| Returns and exchanges | Makes the right notes, with ZATCA's official reason in Arabic and English, and any new draft invoice, from one action on the invoice |
| Summary invoices | Puts a customer's delivery notes for one month on one standard tax invoice, due by the 15th of the next month [VAT Implementing Regulations Art 53(4)] |
| VAT return | Drafts the return box by box, and shows which of its sales ZATCA has accepted, not answered yet or not received, so you can file them first |
| Arabic | Requires Arabic seller and item names, and lists any that are missing |
| ZATCA outage | Queues invoices, retries automatically and records every attempt. The Late reporting page gives you the evidence file and lets you record that you notified ZATCA |
| Records | Issued invoices and their history are never deleted. Backups are encrypted and a yearly copy is kept for fifteen years (see [Security and trust](SECURITY-AND-TRUST.md)) |

## Where things stand

The platform's documents pass ZATCA's official validator. The full onboarding and filing round trip, with all six compliance documents, is accepted in ZATCA's developer sandbox. ZATCA issues simulation and production codes only to a registered taxpayer, so we complete those two steps with each customer during setup.
