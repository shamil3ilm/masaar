Questions
Asked often.
أسئلة شائعة.
Do I need my own ERP?
No. Every invoice can be issued in the portal. If you already have an ERP or an online store, connect it through the API instead.
What happens after the free month?
We agree a plan with you, sized to how many invoices you issue and where you file. Nothing is charged without a plan you have agreed to.
What do I need to sign up?
Your company's legal name, its tax registration and its address, as the authority holds them. The form asks only for what your country's rules need, and says why.
Can I use my own logo and colours?
Yes. An administrator sets your organization's logo and colours in the portal, and your logo is printed on the invoices you issue.
Which languages does it work in?
Arabic and English, in the portal and on every invoice. Each person can switch at any time.
